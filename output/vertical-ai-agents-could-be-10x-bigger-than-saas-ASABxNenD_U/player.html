Tóm tắt

Light Cone podcast (Gary, Jared, Diana) — Jared argues vertical AI agents will be 10x bigger than SaaS. SaaS produced 300+ unicorns in 20 years; vertical AI agents = software + people in one product, replacing payroll not just tools. Three buckets: obvious consumer (incumbents won), non-obvious (Uber, Airbnb), B2B SaaS (300 companies). No "Microsoft of SaaS" — vertical solutions win. Examples: Outset (surveys), MCH (QA), Aprior (recruiting), Cap.ai (dev support), Power (customer support), Salient (voice debt collection). Sell top-down to avoid friction from teams being replaced.
